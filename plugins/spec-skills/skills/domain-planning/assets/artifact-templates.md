# Small records to adapt

Use the project's existing shape first. These prompts describe useful content, not required files or fields. Omit irrelevant portions. Replace prompts with actual reasoning; no completed template proves a sound model.

## Specification index

- Purpose and audience of this collection.
- Reading map: product intent, domain-plan sections, contracts, scenarios, decisions, review guidance.
- Which sources govern which delivery scope, and which remain proposed or superseded.
- Where campaign coordination lives, by reference; no duplicate progress ledger.

## Domain plan

- Purpose and concrete situations, with their source and standing.
- Concepts, shared vocabulary, meaningful relationships/scopes, and why distinctions matter.
- Purpose and responsibility boundaries; behavior and change; relevant invariants.
- Consumer questions, answer paths, and remaining caller interpretation.
- Candidate alternatives, decisions, counterexamples, and unresolved product choices.

## Consequential decision, including compression

- Question and scope; current standing and delivery applicability.
- Choice and rationale; known acceptance basis and what exactly was accepted.
- Enabling assumptions and uncertainty; accepted sacrifice.
- Alternatives or distinctions set aside; tested and excluded scenarios.
- Revision trigger; affected plan/contract sections.
- Supersedes or is superseded by, preserving the former rationale.

## Detailed contract

- Consumer-understandable outcome and affected scope.
- Particular plan/decision sections this contract elaborates.
- Preconditions, outcomes, failure cases, preserved behavior, and non-goals.
- Current versus future obligations and unresolved choices.
- Examples and expected artifacts, what they check, and validation approach.
- Migration impact and affected references when revising semantics.

## Campaign record

- Whether this describes a project undertaking or individual work, and why.
- Objective, bounds, authoritative work-tracking location, and effective convention source.
- Governing plan/contract links; work items, dependencies, progress, and next decision.
- Lasting outcomes promoted into repository knowledge; completion/retention convention.
- A local fallback must say “draft, not synchronized” and identify the intended existing record.

## Local active-work index and checkpoint

The index maps work-item/contributor IDs to distinct checkpoints and campaign links at the configured working area. It is a discovery aid, not project authority.

Each checkpoint records:

- Original job, scope, contributor/work-item identity, and requested result.
- Effective preference source; working location/tracking policy; separate or explicitly shared worktree state.
- Checkout and examined revision; actual edits/checks completed.
- Last meaningful result; live candidate, uncertainty, and pending human choices.
- Relevant project artifact sections/revisions and campaign record, by reference.
- If handing off: smallest dependency, work produced, and concrete resumption condition.
- Next useful action; context that could not be recovered.

Promote specification-shaped and review-needed substance into the repository collection. Checkpoints should not become a hidden second specification. Reconcile their claims with current files on resumption.
